CarTV - Screen Share

iPhone & iPad privacy policy · Updated 9 November 2026

Privacy

CarTV - Screen Share is provided by Soner Capoglu (DBESOFT). This policy covers the iOS app, bundle com.titanviewer.play. No CarTV account is required. The iOS app has no advertisements, advertising identifiers, tracking SDKs or developer-operated analytics.

Your media stays under your control

You choose your files, playlists and media servers. CarTV does not supply TV channels, films or a media subscription. Library records, server addresses and source credentials are stored on your device using CryptoKit encryption and a device-only Keychain key. Imported files, favorites and playback progress are stored locally. Connection details are sent directly to the media source you configure to request your content; that provider has its own privacy policy. HTTPS sources encrypt their network connection. HTTP sources do not.

Screen and audio sharing

Broadcasting starts only after you request it and approve the iOS screen-broadcast prompt. The selected screen can include other apps, photos, videos and notifications. CarTV processes the broadcast on your device and sends it to the selected CarPlay display, AirPlay destination or local receiver. The developer does not receive or store your broadcast. Microphone recording is disabled. A source app may restrict protected video or audio. Stop sharing from CarTV or the iOS broadcast controls.

Permissions

The camera is used only when you open the QR scanner. The system file picker gives access to files you select. Local-network permission supports discovering and communicating with receivers and media sources. Screen capture always uses iOS consent. CarTV does not request your contacts or precise location.

Usage and purchases

The daily viewing allowance and cached Premium entitlement are stored locally and shared with the broadcast extension. They are not sent to a CarTV server. Apple processes purchases, subscription renewals and restoration through StoreKit; CarTV does not receive your payment-card details. Monthly access remains unlimited while the verified subscription is active. Lifetime access is a non-consumable purchase without a time expiry; refunds or revocations remove access. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and choices

You can remove library items and sources in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local application files. Purchase records remain with Apple and can be restored. Manage or cancel auto-renewing subscriptions in your Apple account; uninstalling CarTV does not cancel a subscription. Broadcasting buffers are temporary. If you contact support, the information you choose to email is used to answer your request and is not used for advertising. This public support page is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process normal website request information.

Terms

Use media you own or are authorized to access. The app is licensed under Apple's Standard End User License Agreement. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple services.

Support

Email sonercapogluapp@gmail.com for help or privacy requests. Include your app version, iPhone/iPad model, iOS version and receiver model when relevant. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private broadcast recordings.

To share your screen, connect the receiver, open CarTV and choose Start broadcast, then approve iOS consent. On CarPlay, open CarTV on the car display. Turn your phone sideways for landscape content. Connection and display support depend on your devices and software. To restore Premium, open Premium and choose Restore purchases.

Medyanın kontrolü sende

Dosyaları, oynatma listelerini ve medya sunucularını sen seçersin. CarTV kanal, film veya içerik aboneliği sağlamaz. Kitaplık kayıtları, sunucu adresleri ve kaynak kimlik bilgileri cihazında CryptoKit şifrelemesi ve cihaza özel Keychain anahtarıyla saklanır. İçe aktarılan dosyalar, favoriler ve oynatma ilerlemesi yereldir. HTTPS bağlantıları şifrelidir; HTTP bağlantıları şifreli değildir.

Ekran ve ses yayını

Yayın yalnızca sen başlattığında ve iOS ekran yayını penceresini onayladığında açılır. Ekran diğer uygulamaları, fotoğrafları, videoları ve bildirimleri içerebilir. Görüntü cihazında işlenir ve seçtiğin CarPlay ekranına, AirPlay hedefine veya yerel alıcıya aktarılır. Geliştirici yayını almaz ve saklamaz. Mikrofon kaydı kapalıdır. Kaynak uygulama korumalı görüntü veya sesi kısıtlayabilir. Yayını CarTV veya iOS yayın denetimlerinden durdurabilirsin.

İzinler

Kamera yalnızca QR okuyucuyu açtığında kullanılır. Sistem dosya seçicisi seçtiğin dosyalara erişim sağlar. Yerel ağ izni, alıcıları ve medya kaynaklarını bulmak ve onlarla iletişim kurmak içindir. Ekran kaydı iOS onayı gerektirir. CarTV kişi listeni veya kesin konumunu istemez.

Kullanım süresi ve satın alma

Günlük kullanım süresi ve Premium erişim kaydı cihazında tutulur ve yayın uzantısıyla paylaşılır; CarTV sunucusuna gönderilmez. Ödemeler, abonelik yenilemeleri ve geri yüklemeler Apple StoreKit üzerinden yapılır. CarTV kart bilgilerini almaz. Doğrulanmış aylık abonelik etkinken erişim sınırsızdır.
